Record my try

Put the phone 3-4 m back with the whole wall in view - never on the landing zone. A high beep for each hold in order, a low buzz for a hold not on the route, a double beep for out of order, a tune at the top.

Body tracking uses an AI model (MediaPipe Pose) on the laptop. No video is saved and nothing leaves the laptop. It only checks which holds your hands touch - it does not judge whether a movement is safe. Only film people who agreed to it.
