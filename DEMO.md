# Demo script — Project Ascent

About 2 minutes. Cues only — glance, don't read.

## Before the pitch (10 min earlier)

- Laptop plugged in, camera works: `python ascent.py selftest`
- Panel scanned today, at least 2 routes saved: `python ascent.py list`
- Start live, check it locks: `python ascent.py live --wall panel --train sloper`
- Press **f** for full screen on the projector
- Backup video ready to play: `ascent_demo_*.mp4`
- Markers in your pocket

## On stage

**1 · The problem (15 s)**
- LED board + wall: ~$13,750 (Grasshopper retail)
- Projectors: shadows, dark rooms
- Every climber already has a camera

**2 · Point at the panel (20 s)**
- Rings sit on the real holds
- Walk sideways → rings stay put
- Green start, blue middle, red finish — the LED-board convention climbers know

**3 · The demo moment (20 s)**
- "Lena wants to train slopers"
- Press **g** → new route appears on the wall
- Rules, not AI → every move within reach, always explainable

**4 · Honesty (15 s)**
- Turn the camera away
- Rings disappear, screen says where to look
- "It never guesses. A wrong overlay is worse than none."

**5 · How it works for real (20 s)**
- Scan once: photo, 4 corners, tap holds
- Every visit after: just point the camera
- Holds reset? It notices, asks, archives the old routes

**6 · Next (15 s)**
- Phone app
- Movement feedback — partner, e.g. ClimbAI
- ArtRock owns the data, not a third party (Kilter lesson)

## If something fails

- **No lock** → step back, show the whole panel
- **Still nothing** → play the backup video, keep talking
- **Laptop dies** → design canvas on a phone, same story
- Never debug on stage
